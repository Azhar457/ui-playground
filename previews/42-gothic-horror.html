The Tale

A Gothic Chronicle of Shadows & Bone
~ Chapter I ~
I

It was a night of unnatural stillness. The moon, veiled behind thin clouds like a drowned pearl, cast no shadow upon the earth. The wind had died among the yew trees, and the only sound was the slow, deliberate ticking of the longcase clock in the hall — a sound that seemed to measure not time, but the distance between breath and death.

The door stood ajar. It had been left thus for three nights, though no servant would confess to it. Beyond it lay the west corridor, its walls hung with portraits of men whose eyes followed you with a patience that belonged not to canvas, but to flesh waiting in the dark.

The Dripping

Three drops fell. Then silence. Then three more.

The Final Vigil

"And so the candle burned low, and the shadows gathered round the bed. The figure beneath the shroud did not stir — had not stirred for seven days. Yet when the clock struck midnight, the flame bent eastward, as if in answer to a call from the moor."
