IMPERFECTION IS
THE SIGNATURE. IMPERFECTION IS
THE SIGNATURE.

RISO·LAB is an independent two-drum press refusing the myth of pixel-perfect reproduction. We set our registration intentionally off — every third pass slips, every sheet's ink kiss is unique, and no two copies of an issue leave the drying rack identical. That drift is not a defect; it is the physical record of the machine's hand.

Our output is deliberately small: editions cap at three hundred impressions because a risograph is a mimeograph descendant, closer to letterpress and stone lithography than to digital toner. We treat each drum pass as a spot-color decision, halftones screened at opposing angles to hold shadow detail without moiré, and soy inks pressed into rough uncoated stock that drinks the color.

The lab runs on scavenged drums, rebuilt gears, and a decade of accumulated roller-slip tables. Every run is an experiment; every misregistered overprint is filed in the archive as a legitimate plate, not a failed state.

— THE RISO·LAB COLLECTIVE, EDITION 051
