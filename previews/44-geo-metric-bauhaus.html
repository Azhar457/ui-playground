GEOMETRY

⊞ Isometric System

Modular vertices arranged on an orthogonal grid. Each node connects to form a continuous mesh — no gaps, no overlaps. Pure parametric structure driven by mathematical modulus and vector logic.

Core Elements

Four fundamental constructs of the geometric system

⊞ Vertex

The atomic unit of geometric space. A singular point that anchors every line, every curve, every polygon in the system.

◇ Node

Connective tissue of the grid. Where vertices meet and branch — the intersection that holds the entire tessellation together.

◆ Mesh

Planar faces stitched edge-to-edge. A continuous surface where every polygon shares its boundaries with neighbors.

△ Vector

Directed force across the plane. Magnitude and orientation — the engine that extrudes flat polygons into dimensional space.

◈ Tessellation Engine

Modular tiling of the plane using congruent polygons. Each tile is a vertex-defined shape that repeats without gaps or overlaps — a periodic tiling derived from isometric projection grids. The system supports regular, semi-regular, and non-periodic tessellations controlled by parametric modulus values.
