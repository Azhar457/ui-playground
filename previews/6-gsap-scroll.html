Step 01 / Narrative Focus

The Problem of Scroll Syncing.

Traditional narrative websites fade components on scroll arbitrarily, fighting the natural pacing. We need scrolling to drive timelines directly.

Step 02 / The Mechanism

Horizontal Translation inside Vertical space.

GSAP ScrollTrigger pins the viewport at y-coordinates. Scrolling down shifts the slider horizontally across X-coordinates, tying layout to fingers.
